# Public Plugin Store Comparison

Snapshot date: 2026-05-17  
Scope: public OKX OnchainOS Plugin Store trading plugins visible from public pages.

## Summary

The strongest public trading plugins tend to sell one of three stories:

- fast meme discovery and automated execution;
- wallet / smart-money copy trading;
- dashboard-backed strategy or market analysis.

Quoteable Smart-Money Meme Scout should not imitate their most aggressive live-trading claims. Its best lane is different: **execution-realism and auditability before action**.

## Comparison Matrix

| Plugin | Public Positioning | Strength | Gap / Risk | Our Differentiation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `meme-trench-scanner` | Scans 11 launchpads every 10 seconds, uses TX acceleration, volume surge, safety checks, and 7-layer exits. | Very strong speed and live-bot story. | Aggressive automation is harder to audit from outside; real quote/exit-route evidence is not the headline. | We lead with fresh entry quote + fresh exit route evidence and explicit review artifacts. |
| `smart-money-signal-copy-trade` | Polls OKX Smart Money / KOL / Whale signals every 20 seconds, requires co-rider consensus, has 15 safety filters and 7-layer exits. | Strong smart-money narrative and dashboard/hot-reload story. | Copy-trade consensus can still fail on routeability, slippage, and late entry. | We can use smart-money as a source, but require quoteability and risk evidence before paper action. |
| `wallet-tracker-mcap` | Tracks target wallets, mirrors trades with MC target or instant follow, 4-tier risk, 5-trigger exits. | Simple product story: follow wallets, manage exits. | Requires trusted wallet lists and can inherit source-wallet mistakes. | We are not dependent on one wallet list; every token must pass quote/risk/route checks. |
| `top-rank-tokens-sniper` | Scans OKX 1h gainers Top 20 every 10 seconds; uses safety checks and rank-out exit. | Clear momentum/sniper story and very easy to understand. | Leaderboard chasing may buy late; rank-out is clean but can be noisy. | We do not chase rank alone; quoteability, exit route, holder/security evidence, and audit logs come first. |
| `starter-coach` | Conversational strategy-builder that guides user through backtest, paper trade, and go-live confirmation. | Great user onboarding and safety flow. | More general-purpose, less competition-specific edge. | Our Skill is narrower and more contest-specific: Solana/X Layer meme scouting with real competition evidence. |
| `mainstream-spot-order` | Multi-chain mainstream token strategy with OHLCV, TA ensemble, backtesting, dashboard, and live mode. | Strong traditional strategy / dashboard package. | Less focused on meme-token routeability and holder/security risks. | Our Skill is tailored to meme competition realities: risk tags, bundler/top-holder/promotion, and exit-route checks. |

## Where We Are Stronger

- Cleaner review evidence: candidate report, risk audit log, paper ledger, post-trade review, live evidence summary.
- Safer public package: no private keys, no wallet export, no live Skill execution, no Telegram router.
- Better execution-realism story: no fresh quote and no exit route means no paper candidate.
- Better honesty around live evidence: operator-in-the-loop trades are useful evidence, but not overstated as autonomous Skill execution.

## Where Competitors Look Stronger

- They present as runnable live bots, often with dashboards.
- They use crisp install/start flows such as `npx skills add ...` plus `python3 bot.py`.
- Some have clearer active-monitoring loops and hot-reload configuration.

## Packaging Response

To match OKX's taste while staying honest, this repo emphasizes:

1. OnchainOS-first primary source.
2. Paper-first safety onboarding.
3. Deterministic risk gates and exits.
4. Fresh quote + both-side route checks.
5. Operator-in-the-loop live competition evidence.
6. Validator and demo that run in under a minute.
